# 2. Literature Novelty Audit — Primary Sources & Honest Positioning

**Core editorial rule:** Do **not** claim novelty for (i) factor–profile covariance equivalence, (ii) minimum-rank factor analysis / the Frisch problem, (iii) the Ledermann dimension bound, (iv) the classical pentad polynomial, or (v) the abstract possibility that continuous distributions produce clusters (Costa et al., 2002; Bauer & Curran, 2003; Katahira et al., 2020).

---

## Primary-Source Verification Summary

1. **Factor / Latent Profile Covariance Equivalence:**
   - **Molenaar & von Eye (1994)**, *"On the arbitrary nature of latent variables"*, in *Latent Variables Analysis: Applications for Developmental Research* (SAGE), and **Bartholomew (1987)** established that continuous factor models and discrete latent class/profile models can reproduce identical first- and second-order moments (covariances), making model choice arbitrary at the covariance level alone.
   - **Muthén (2004)**, *The SAGE Handbook of Quantitative Methodology for the Social Sciences*, Ch. 19, §19.3.4.2 (`https://www.statmodel.com/download/KaplanChapter19.pdf`), summarizes this covariance equivalence explicitly in the growth-mixture / LPA setting.
2. **Frisch Scheme & Minimum-Rank Factor Analysis (MRFA):**
   - **Frisch (1934)** introduced the problem of decomposing $R = D + B$ with diagonal $D \succeq 0$ and minimal $\operatorname{rank}(B)$; **ten Berge & Kiers (1991)** and **Shapiro & ten Berge (2002)** developed MRFA algorithms and theory; **Ning, Georgiou, Tannenbaum & Boyd (2015)** (*SIAM Review*, 57, 167–197, DOI `10.1137/130921179`) provide the modern convex/algebraic review of the Frisch scheme and highlight that minimum trace (MTFA) is **not** in general equivalent to minimum rank.
3. **Ledermann Bound & Rank-Reducibility:**
   - **Ledermann (1937)** proposed the rank-reduction bound; **Shapiro (1982)** (*Psychometrika*, 47, 187–199, DOI `10.1007/BF02296274`) proved rigorously that the set of symmetric matrices reducible to rank $r$ has positive Lebesgue measure iff $r \ge \lceil (2p + 1 - \sqrt{8p+1})/2 \rceil$.
4. **Tetrads & Pentads in Algebraic Factor Analysis:**
   - **Drton, Sturmfels & Sullivant (2007)** (*Probab. Theory Relat. Fields*, 138, 463–493; `arXiv:math/0509390`) proved that the vanishing ideal of $5 \times 5$ rank-2 factor covariance matrices is generated by the classical 12-term degree-5 **pentad** (Kelley, 1928; Harman, 1976), while emphasizing that $P(R) = 0$ is necessary but not sufficient for real positive-semidefinite factorability with $D \succ 0$.
5. **Simplex Moments, Multivariate Skewness, & Cubature:**
   - **Mardia (1970)** (*Biometrika*, 57, 519–530) defined multivariate skewness $\beta_{1,r}(Z) = \mathbb{E}[(Z^\top Z')^3] = \|\cum_3(Z)\|_F^2$ for standardized $Z$.
   - **Stroud (1971)** (*Approximate Calculation of Multiple Integrals*) and **Mysovskikh (1968)** established that a degree-3 cubature formula in $\mathbb{R}^r$ for a centrally symmetric measure (matching zero third moments) requires at least $2r$ points, ruling out $(r+1)$-point simplices for $r \ge 2$.
   - **Priority assessment of Theorem 2 ($\|T\|_F^2 = \sum_{k=1}^{r+1}\pi_k^{-1} - 3(r+1) + 2 \ge r(r-1)$):** Explicitly derived here from simplex Gram geometry and cumulant additivity. Its qualitative consequence (that $(r+1)$-point isotropic simplices have nonzero third moments for $r \ge 2$) aligns with Mardia skewness and degree-3 cubature theory; however, we have **not** established whether this exact closed-form weight-dependent Frobenius norm identity and its homoscedastic-LPA corollary ($K_{\mathrm{diag,hom}}^{(3)}(R) \ge r_+(R) + 2$) appear verbatim in prior mixture-model literature. **Priority is therefore stated conservatively as uncertain pending specialist review.**
6. **Personality Typology Debate:**
   - **Costa et al. (2002)**, **Bauer & Curran (2003)**, **Gerlach et al. (2018)**, **Freudenstein et al. (2019)**, **Gerlach, Revelle & Amaral (2019)** (*Nat. Hum. Behav.*, 3, 1047–1048, DOI `10.1038/s41562-019-0722-3` — verified), and **Katahira et al. (2020)** (already demonstrated unimodal skewed enrichment under Gerlach's procedure).
