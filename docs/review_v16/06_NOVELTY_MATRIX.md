# P3 — Evidence-Based Novelty Matrix

**Framing principle:** Do **not** claim first-ever proof that clusters arise from continuous distributions (Costa et al., 2002 and Bauer & Curran, 2003 already demonstrated this empirically/via simulation). Instead, ground novelty in a **unified, analytically proved, empirically calibrated inference framework** spanning five domains: (a) exact geometric quantization & density-ratio identities, (b) 5D direct topographic mapping of Gerlach et al.'s enriched centres with calibrated positive controls, (c) matched $2 \times 2$ continuous-null decomposition + optimizer/representation sensitivity, (d) cross-cultural individual-level generalization to psychopathology (HiTOP-SR), and (e) leak-free out-of-sample predictive utility across 86 life outcomes.
